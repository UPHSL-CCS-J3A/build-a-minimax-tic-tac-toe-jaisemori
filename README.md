What is Minimax?
The Minimax algorithm is a method used in artificial intelligence to help computers make smart decisions in games. It is commonly used in two-player games such as chess, tic-tac-toe, or checkers. The main idea behind Minimax is to choose the best possible move while assuming that the opponent is also playing their best. There are two players — one is called the Maximizer and the other is the Minimizer. The Maximizer tries to get the highest possible score, while the Minimizer tries to make that score as low as possible. The algorithm looks at all the possible moves each player can make and predicts what might happen next.

Minimax gives a score. A good move for the Maximizer will have a high score, and a good move for the Minimizer will have a low score. The algorithm goes through the game tree, which shows all possible future moves, and works backward to find the best move. When it’s the Maximizer’s turn, it picks the move with the highest score. When it’s the Minimizer’s turn, it picks the move with the lowest score. The Minimax algorithm helps a player choose a move that gives the best guaranteed outcome, even if the opponent plays perfectly. In simple terms, it allows the computer to “think ahead” and prepare for the opponent’s best response. The Minimax algorithm is an important part of artificial intelligence in gaming. It helps computers make logical, strategic decisions by looking ahead and considering every possible move. This makes games more challenging and allows machines to play almost like real humans.
